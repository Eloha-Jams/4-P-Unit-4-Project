Christmas Trees and The different types.

When it comes to tree types there are Two
distinct categories you have to lookout for;
Natural and Artificial. Within these
categories there are multiple different types of
trees you can choose from. So for your convenience I
will list 3 each the Best and Affordable types of Chritmas trees and Where you can Buy them! Before we get started I would like to say that Everybody is entitled to their own opinions. I am
not here to dictate what you do your home, I believe
that no one type of tree is better than the other and
that both the Natural and Artificial trees have their pros and cons.

Natural Trees

  • Douglas Fir Tree
    are widely known as one of the best Trees you can buy For that nostalgic Christmas look. Douglas Trees are Most notable Their soft needles which are dark green There are needles that radiate in all directions from the branch As well Classic Christmas shape.
    For example


  • Fraser Fir Trees
    Are one of the more Popular Christmas tree types. Their most notable characteristics being That their branches turn slightly upward They have a great form and needle retention as well as their dark blue and green colors as well as A pleasant smell.
    For example


  • Noble Fir Tree
    Are considered an Excellent Christmas tree because of their beauty. Their most common characteristics are there upturned needles, Longevity, and stiff branches, as well as they provide excellent greenery for wreaths and garlands.
    For example



Artificial Trees

  • Full Trees
    As the name suggests are a more robust type of artificial tree giving you the full effect of a natural Christmas tree.
    For example


  • Slim Trees
    are great trees for when you have a limited amount of space. Though they are slimmer they are taller which still gives you a lot of room to decorate.
    For example


  • Flatback Trees
    are great trees for apartments or small homes. It gives the illusion of an entire Christmas tree while preserving a lot of space.
    For example
