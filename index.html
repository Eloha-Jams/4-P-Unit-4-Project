A Theme to Remember!

The first thing you have to understand about
Christmas is that a theme is everything!
Since there are so many themes to choose from,
it can be absolutly overwhelming.
But! You don't have to worry, because I have a list of my top 5 best Christmas themes!
  • A Nostalgic Christmas
    for more sentimental people who want that classic childhood Christmas.

  • An "Object" Christmas
    is when you take an element
    of Christmas like Gingerbread
    or Candycanes and build your Christmas decor around it.
    For example


  • A Woodland Christmas
    is when you take a more Nature inspired approach to capture the quiet and rustic beauty of a snow covered winter forest.
    For example


  • A Diy Christmas
    is mainly a Christmas for the crafters and is where you make most if not all decorations from scratch, showing off your creativity and your determination for a fully do-it-yourself Christmas.
    For example


  • A "Monochrome" Christmas
    as the name suggests is where you take one specific color and build your entire atmosphere around it, and going so far as to even having the same colored tree.

Christmas Trees and The different types.

When it comes to tree types there are Two distinct categories you have to lookout for; Natural and Artificial. Within these categories there are multiple different types of trees you can choose from.So for your convenience I will list 3 of the Best and Affordable types of Chritmas trees and where you can buy them! Before we get started I would like to say that Everybody is entitled to their own opinions. I am not here to dictate what you do your home, I believe that no one type of tree is better than the other and both the Natural and Artificial trees have their pros and cons.
